Our Installation Process

1. Site Assessment

We inspect the installation location, available utilities, foundation requirements and machine specifications.

2. Machine Positioning

Machines are positioned accurately, levelled and securely anchored for optimum performance.

3. Electrical & Mechanical Installation

Our engineers complete electrical wiring, control panel connections and mechanical assembly.

4. Testing & Commissioning

The machine is tested under operating conditions before being handed over to the client.
