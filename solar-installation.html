Our Solar Installation Process

1. Site Survey & Energy Assessment

We evaluate your energy consumption, roof space, installation location and future power requirements to design the most suitable solar system.

2. System Design

Our engineers design a complete solar solution, including panel layout, inverter sizing, battery capacity and electrical protection.

3. Installation

4. Testing & Commissioning

The entire solar system is tested, inspected and commissioned to ensure safe, efficient and reliable operation before handover.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do solar panels last?

Quality solar panels typically have a lifespan of 25 years or more when properly installed and maintained.


Can solar work during cloudy weather?

Yes. Solar panels continue to generate electricity during cloudy conditions, although output is lower than on sunny days.


Do I need batteries?

Not always. On-grid systems may operate without batteries, while off-grid and hybrid systems use batteries for energy storage and backup power.


Do you provide maintenance services?

Yes. We offer inspection, cleaning, troubleshooting, performance testing and preventive maintenance for solar energy systems.
